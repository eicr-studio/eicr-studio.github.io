EICRL is based in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at UNSW Sydney. Choose the path that best matches your enquiry so we can respond with the right context.

## Research collaboration

For academic collaboration, joint proposals, visiting-researcher discussions or shared experimental work, briefly describe the research question, the connection to our [research themes](/research/) and the contribution you have in mind.

<a class="content-action" href="mailto:o.obst@unsw.edu.au?subject=EICRL%20research%20collaboration">Discuss a research collaboration</a>

## Prospective research students

PhD and MPhil enquiries need more information than a general contact message. Start with the [Join the lab](/join/) page, which explains research fit, funding, start windows, required material and the enquiry process.

<a class="content-action content-action-secondary" href="/join/">Read the prospective-researcher guide</a>

## Industry and applied research

We welcome conversations about robotics, intelligent physical systems, research translation, shared platforms and longer-term university–industry collaboration. Include the operational problem, the intended setting and the kind of engagement you are considering.

<a class="content-action" href="mailto:o.obst@unsw.edu.au?subject=EICRL%20industry%20enquiry">Discuss an industry problem</a>

## Media, talks and general enquiries

For media comment, seminars, invited talks, demonstrations or enquiries that do not fit the categories above, include the organisation, topic and relevant date.

<a class="content-action" href="mailto:o.obst@unsw.edu.au?subject=EICRL%20general%20enquiry">Send a general enquiry</a>
