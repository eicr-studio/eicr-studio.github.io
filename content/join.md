EICRL welcomes enquiries from prospective PhD and MPhil researchers whose interests connect closely with our work in robotics, machine learning and intelligent physical systems. A good enquiry starts with research fit: the problem you want to study, the experience you bring and why this lab is the right place to develop the work.

## Current opportunities

We are open to well-matched expressions of interest. Supervision availability depends on research fit, timing, project resources and the current capacity of the supervisory team. Unless a specific funded project is advertised, an initial discussion is not an offer of admission or funding.

The strongest enquiries usually connect to one of our current directions:

- [data-efficient embodied learning and manipulation](/research/#data-efficient-embodied-learning-for-manipulation)
- [collective robotics under physical uncertainty](/research/#collective-robotics-under-physical-uncertainty)
- [learning and uncertainty for physical systems](/research/#learning-and-uncertainty-for-physical-systems)

Review our [current projects](/projects/) and [selected publications](/publications/) before getting in touch.

## Degrees, admission and funding

UNSW offers several higher degree research pathways, including the PhD, Master by Research and Master of Philosophy. Admission and scholarship assessment are related but separate processes.

Applicants normally identify a potential supervisor, discuss a viable research direction, prepare the required research-plan material and then submit a complete application through the [UNSW Higher Degree Research application process](https://www.unsw.edu.au/research/hdr/application).

Funding can come from Australian Government, UNSW, faculty or school, industry, international-partner or external sources. Some awards include a stipend and tuition support, but scholarships are competitive and a strong academic record does not guarantee funding. International applicants must also show how fees and living costs will be covered. Check the current [UNSW HDR scholarships](https://www.unsw.edu.au/research/hdr/scholarships) and [fees and costs](https://www.unsw.edu.au/research/hdr/fees-and-costs) information before planning an application.

## Start windows and timing

The UNSW HDR calendar has four annual commencement periods: Summer Term and Terms 1–3. The realistic start window for an individual applicant depends on admission processing, scholarship rounds, supervision, project readiness and—where relevant—visa timing.

Use the current [HDR academic calendar and application deadlines](https://www.unsw.edu.au/research/hdr/application) rather than relying on dates copied into an email. Allow several months between an initial conversation and a possible start, particularly when scholarship assessment or international relocation is involved.

## What to send

Please include:

- a concise explanation of the research problem or direction that interests you
- why that direction fits EICRL's current work
- your academic background, degree results and relevant technical skills
- your research experience, including thesis, publications or substantial project work
- your likely funding route: existing support, external application, UNSW scholarship or self-funding
- a CV and academic transcript
- a thesis abstract, writing sample, publication, portfolio or code link where available
- your preferred degree and approximate start period

A short, specific message is more useful than a generic proposal. It should show that you have read the relevant research and can explain a plausible connection between your previous work and the proposed direction.

## What happens next

1. Review the research themes, projects and formal UNSW requirements.
2. Send a focused expression of interest with the material above.
3. If the fit and timing appear promising, we can discuss the research direction, supervision and resources.
4. A suitable applicant then prepares the formal UNSW admission and, where relevant, scholarship application.

Only the university can issue an admission or scholarship offer. Formal eligibility, English-language requirements, documents and deadlines are set centrally by UNSW and may change.
